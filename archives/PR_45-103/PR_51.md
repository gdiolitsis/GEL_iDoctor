## 31 Labs Turbo — Lab 13 Bluetooth evidence safety

**Independent Draft PR**, stacked on PR #46 frozen head `363dece0aec9c9c032c3cfa1bfbdf7380dbda38e`. No edits to parallel Lab 6/8/11/12 Draft PRs (#47–#50), `main`, manifest, permissions or Play publishing.

### Source-confirmed defects
- The ACL receiver handled **CONNECTED only** and never set `lab13ReceiverSawDisconnection`, losing link-change evidence between polls.
- `getProfileConnectionState` is adapter-wide/aggregate and is **not evidence of a particular connected accessory**, measured GATT payload or RF health.
- Accessory wait had no timeout; the prior "60s" window used callback tick counts, not monotonic elapsed duration.
- Logs disclosed paired-device **names and MAC addresses** to the service report without diagnostic necessity.
- Interrupted/backgrounded runs, missing permissions, absence of a peripheral or adapter read errors could yield misleading hardware-like verdicts or incomplete cleanup/export.

### Patch
- New pure-Java `Lab13BluetoothEvidence` classifier distinguishes `NOT_TESTED`, `NO_ACCESSORY_OBSERVED`, `INCOMPLETE`, `ADAPTER_UNAVAILABLE`, `AGGREGATE_LINK_OBSERVED`, `AGGREGATE_LINK_CHANGES_OBSERVED`. There is no hardware PASS/FAIL.
- ACL CONNECT and DISCONNECT handling, ambiguous simultaneous transitions tracked separately, explicit scope warning when multiple peripherals are present.
- Monotonic elapsed 60s sample and bounded 45s accessory wait, session generation and callback cleanup.
- Canceled/backgrounded runs tear down receiver and handler and cannot become an old "stable" session; report export restored.
- Count-only paired-device metadata; **no MAC/address/name in logs**. Null/unavailable OS state is not hardware fault.
- **10 new Java regression tests** and **20 negative-control and real-device cases**, documented at `docs/qa/LAB13_BLUETOOTH_EVIDENCE_ACCEPTANCE_2026-11-09.md`.
- No auto-pair, Bluetooth scanning, GATT traffic, new permissions or invasive RF stress.
