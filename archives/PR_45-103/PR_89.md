## Real Xiaomi observation
Lab 7 verified both stable portrait→landscape orientation and timestamped FAR→NEAR→FAR proximity sequence, yet the logs displayed a yellow "Display power was not measured; call-screen blanking is unverified" warning. This was a false alarm: Lab 7's `ProximityCheckActivity` explicitly does not perform a live-call screen-off test.

- Remove repetitive generic "Όριο διάγνωσης / Diagnostic limit" boilerplate from the complete-coverage success branch, consistent with the already accepted Lab 6 log policy.
- Preserve measured sensor sources, orientation verification, timestamped FAR→NEAR→FAR, all partial/unverified/cancelled warnings, the guided sequence and `Lab7SensorEvidence` algorithms without changes.
- The scope limitations remain explicit in `ProximityCheckActivity` source and its on-screen instructions. No in-call blanking certification is asserted.

## Discussion: no intrusive screen-off automation
Do not add a synthetic proximity wake-lock test: it would be testing the app's own screen-off request, **not** whether the OEM phone app correctly blanks during a real call. Avoid forced display power changes, new permissions, false positives, and lifecycle risks. Optional real-call checks can be considered as separately user-reported evidence, never as automated sensor proof.
