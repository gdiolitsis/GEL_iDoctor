Isolated **Draft PR**, stacked on the unchanged #46 head `363dece0aec9c9c032c3cfa1bfbdf7380dbda38e`. Independent of Draft #47 (touch), #48 (camera) and #49 (cellular). **No change to main, Play, manifest/permissions, billing or backup.**

### Confirmed source-level limitations
- Previous Lab12 only observed SIM/service and a generic default-network `NET_CAPABILITY_INTERNET`, mistakenly labeled Internet as available although this neither verifies validated connectivity nor IMS/VoLTE/VoWiFi calling.
- Missing/denied service API reads were indistinguishable from `!inService` in shared snapshot.
- No real call was performed or confirmed; nevertheless a broad "Lab12 finished" report was emitted.

### Implemented improvement
- Adds independent, source-limited Android observations of airplane mode, reported voice capability (`isDeviceVoiceCapable` on API35+), default SIM state, and **known/unknown legacy service status**.
- No inference of real-call success from Wi-Fi/VPN/IMS speculation.
- Optional, user-consented **empty `Intent.ACTION_DIAL`** without auto-calling, prefilled number, new permissions, call logs or recorded audio. User alone chooses whether to call and whom.
- On return, user reports actual call completion, whether they heard the other party and whether the other party heard them. Even a positive two-way result is `USER_REPORTED_TWO_WAY_AUDIO` — not independently verified modem, earpiece, microphone, route, radio or IMS health.
- `NOT_TESTED` / `INCONCLUSIVE` on missing or interrupted information; explicit negative report is an uncorroborated one-way issue, not a hardware failure.
- `Lab12CallEvidence.java` pure classifier and **12 JUnit scenarios**.
- **17 falsification/device acceptance controls** in `docs/qa/LAB12_CALL_EVIDENCE_ACCEPTANCE_2026-11-09.md`.
