## Confirmed source issue
`ManualTestsActivity` computed a legacy `dynamic resistance` proxy from voltage sag divided by **absolute net battery current** (not a synchronized `ΔI`) and displayed fabricated Excellent/Good/Normal/High battery-cell quality tiers. The displayed `energy efficiency` was actually consumption rate (mAh/h) divided by sag (V), giving units mAh/(h·V) rather than an energy-efficiency metric.

## Minimal correction without removing functionality
- Retain both existing calculated numerical observations, experimental/stress test control flow, session management, sampling, output panels and export operations.
- Replace only the **misleading user-facing verdict labels**: show exploratory ΔV/|I| ratio (mΩ proxy only; NOT measured cell ESR/SoH), and exploratory discharge-rate/sag ratio (mAh/(h·V); NOT energy efficiency). Remove uncalibrated Excellent/Good/Normal/High and Efficient/Normal/Inefficient classifications from these two outputs.
- Add `Lab14DerivedRatioPolicy` and five JUnit tests asserting no calibrated hardware-health assertion without paired current delta.
- No new permissions, no stress load changes, no deletion of Lab 14 functions, no main merge.
