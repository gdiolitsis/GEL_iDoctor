Historical Lab 28 combined *words from unstructured logs* to assert PMIC/baseband/NAND damage, sensor bus failure, and motherboard risk — not valid physical evidence. We do **not** restore those unsupported automatic verdicts.

Instead, preserve and extend **observable time-series value** within the Android SDK:
- Existing public Android thermal status/headroom, battery temperature, reported voltage/current, cycles and fresh Lab14 observations are unchanged.
- Capture an opt-in user-initiated repeat observation as paired Android-reported voltage and temperature, locally stored. Compare two samples separated by **30 seconds–2 hours** on the same boot; reject stale, mismatched clock, missing or unavailable readings.
- Label exact signed differences in mV/°C with timeframe and provenance; do **not** infer PMIC, board, NAND, RF, repair history, or physical battery health.
- Preserve first observation when the operator rechecks too soon; never overwrite with all-empty samples.
- New pure `Lab28TemporalEvidence` and seven tests cover one sample, too-soon, valid signed delta, missing one metric, missing both, reboot/time change, stale evidence.
- [ ] JUnit on exact head; [ ] downstream Lab30/31/PDF structured evidence; [ ] physical repeatability across devices and load conditions at a later integration milestone.
- **No merge, no per-lab APK build and no Play rollout without owner approval.**
