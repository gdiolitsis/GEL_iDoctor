## 31 Labs Turbo — Lab 11 mobile-network evidence

Isolated **Draft**, stacked on the unchanged PR #46 (`363dece0aec9c9c032c3cfa1bfbdf7380dbda38e`). Independent of Draft PR #47 (Lab6) and #48 (Lab8); main/Play/permissions unchanged.

### Source-confirmed false positive
Old Lab11 could claim "mobile network is operating normally" when a READY SIM merely coexisted with Wi-Fi or VPN Internet capability; Android `NET_CAPABILITY_INTERNET` is not `VALIDATED` connectivity and its default transport need not be cellular. Failed `getServiceState()` defaulted to OUT_OF_SERVICE, while `DATA_DISCONNECTED` was conflated with mobile data switched OFF.

### Proposed fixes
- Typed/provenance-aware default-route classification: direct CELLULAR/Wi-Fi/VPN/mixed, `NET_CAPABILITY_INTERNET` vs `VALIDATED`, captive portal, unknown/no-default states.
- Explicitly distinguish SIM, service read available/unknown, Telephony packet state, cached OS-reported 0–4 signal level and default Internet route.
- Positive **observed default cellular** validation only when Android confirms CELLULAR + VALIDATED without VPN; never modem hardware-health certification.
- Wi-Fi default and VPN underlay are not evidence that the cellular modem works; absent/locked SIM or denied permission is not proof of broken hardware.
- No new permissions, automatic cellular requests, metered probing or hidden APIs. Lab12's legacy consumer is left for an independent audit.
- Pure Java `Lab11NetworkEvidence` + 10 targeted JUnit test cases; `docs/qa/LAB11_CELLULAR_EVIDENCE_ACCEPTANCE_2026-11-08.md` with 15 falsification/device controls.
