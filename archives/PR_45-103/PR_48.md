## 31 Labs Turbo — Lab 8 camera-preview evidence integrity

**Stacked Draft PR** on the unchanged #46 head `363dece0aec9c9c032c3cfa1bfbdf7380dbda38e`. This branch does not touch PR #47 (Lab 6), main, Play Store, permissions or the immutable backup.

### Source-level defects addressed
- Earlier Camera2 5-second sample calculated its final preview verdict **before** the user answered, and a subsequent `I SEE IMAGE` incremented successful-preview count unconditionally.
- A startup error could still enable a subjective YES/NO which turned missing samples into green via the above unconditional counter.
- Replaces these paths with a pure Java `Lab8Evidence` contract: actually started repeating stream, bounded window completed, at least one Camera2 frame and valid luma sample, **then** fresh user confirmation.
- `USER_REPORTED_NO_IMAGE`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, `UNAVAILABLE` are not automatic hardware FAIL.
- Revised summary to count evidence per camera, not certify overall optical hardware, AF, lens or ISP health.
- Torch `setTorchMode()` accepted/failed commands are not physical emission verification.
- Closes camera resources on dismissal/background; bounds startup wait; guards late Camera2 callbacks.
- Added 9 JUnit regressions in `Lab8EvidenceTest.java` and 15 acceptance/negative-control cases in `docs/qa/LAB8_CAMERA_EVIDENCE_ACCEPTANCE_2026-11-08.md`.
